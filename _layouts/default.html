FAQ

Clear answers for the first-time visitor.

What can Clypse identify?

Movies, TV episodes, and famous quotes you say out loud. It recognizes a title from its dialogue, so it does not matter where it is playing — a streaming app, live TV, a cinema or a clip in a social app. What matters is that the title is in our database.

Does it support every movie and show?

No. The database is still expanding and new titles are added every week. When something is missing you can request it from Supported Titles, or let Deep Search look for it on the web.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works signed out. Sign in with Apple is optional and only exists so your saved clips, quotes and game stats can be backed up and synced to your other devices.

What is Deep Search?

A web fallback for titles that are not in our database yet. It uses the dialogue Clypse heard, only counts against your allowance when it finds a match, and gives each clip one deeper retry. Free accounts get 5 successful searches a month, Clypse+ gets 20.

Can I use it on what is playing on my own phone?

Yes. Screen Capture records the audio of whatever is playing on this iPhone for 15–20 seconds, so a clip in a social app or a streaming app works without holding the phone up to anything.

What happens after a match?

You get the title, the episode, the year, genres, ratings, roughly where in the runtime the line was said, and a button for each streaming service carrying it — plus the option to save it, share a link, or tell us whether the match was right.

What if it only gets close?

Clypse shows a short list of possible matches instead of guessing. Tap Correct on the right one, or try Deep Search if none of them fit.

Any tips for better results?

Capture clear dialogue rather than music or effects, keep the room quiet, and aim for 10–15 seconds. With Live Audio, take your headphones out — the mic cannot hear your TV through them — or use Screen Capture instead.
