# Changelog

All notable changes to Clypse are documented here.

## [2.0] — November 2025

### The accounts, sync and Deep Search update
The biggest release since launch. Clypse can now capture what's playing on your own screen, keep looking when a title isn't in our database, and follow you between devices.

**Screen Capture**
* Record the audio of whatever is playing on this iPhone for 15–20 seconds — a streaming app, a clip in a social app, anything — without holding the phone up to a speaker.
* Results survive tab switches, and a floating "Recording…" pill brings you back.

**Leave while it works**
* A Live Activity shows recognition and Deep Search progress on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
* A notification brings you straight back to the result when it's ready.
* Notification permission is now asked for after a screen recording or a daily puzzle, never at launch.

**Deep Search**
* When a title isn't in our database yet, Deep Search looks for it on the web using the dialogue we heard.
* It's only charged when it finds a match, and every clip gets one free "search deeper" retry.
* 5 successful searches a month on the free tier, 20 a month with Clypse+, plus one-off credit packs.

**Possible matches**
* When we're close but not certain, Clypse shows a short list of candidates instead of guessing. Tap **Correct** on the right one.
* A clearer "Couldn't hear a reliable line" state when the audio wasn't usable.

**Accounts and sync (optional)**
* Sign in with Apple to back up your saved clips and quotes and sync them to your other devices — up to 2,000 with Clypse+.
* Game results, stats and streaks are kept per player, and a device's progress merges in the first time you sign in.
* Everything still works signed out, exactly as before. Accounts can be deleted in the app.

**Clypse+**
* $3.99 / month or $29.99 / year through the App Store.
* Every past Framed and Quotele puzzle, requests for future puzzles, 20 Deep Searches a month, and 2,000 clips of backup.
* Deep Search credit packs for anyone who'd rather not subscribe.

**Sharing**
* Share any match as a normal web link (`share.clypseai.com`). Whoever opens it sees the scene, with or without the app installed.

**Results and where to watch**
* One button per streaming service, with the right logo, however the provider is named.
* Results scroll properly on every screen size, and feedback buttons have haptics.

**Settings and help**
* Refreshed Guide, Help, Supported Titles and About.
* Browse the supported movies and shows, or request one that's missing.
* A microphone picker in Settings, and an updated Privacy Policy and Terms of Service covering accounts, Clypse+ and credits.

## [1.1.0] — February 2025

### The gameplay update
Clypse became more than recognition, with daily games to test your movie knowledge.

* **Quotele**: guess the show or movie from a single line of dialogue.
* **Framed**: identify the film from a single frame.
* **Daily streaks**: track your recognition prowess over time.

## [1.0.0] — January 17, 2025

### Initial release

**Clypse** arrived on the App Store.

**Instant audio recognition**
- Record audio from any TV show or movie
- Get identification results in seconds

**Content database**
- Detailed episode information
- Genre classifications and descriptions

**Personal clip library**
- Save your favorite recognized moments
- Organize clips by show or movie
