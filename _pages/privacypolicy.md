# Privacy Policy

Clypse is built to work without an account, without ads, and without tracking you across other apps. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, and how long we keep it.

## Audio and Transcripts

- When you use Live Audio or Screen Capture, the clip is sent to our servers and transcribed by OpenAI.
- The audio file is deleted as soon as it has been transcribed. We keep a fingerprint (hash) of the audio so a repeated clip can be answered quickly. It can't be turned back into audio.
- We keep the transcript and the result (the match, possible matches and how confident we were) as a recognition record. We use these records to run the service, fix problems and improve recognition.
- Clypse only records when you start it. Live Audio needs your permission to use the microphone, and Screen Capture runs only while you're broadcasting.

## Device Identifier

Clypse creates a random identifier for your device. It isn't your advertising identifier, your name or your phone number. We use it to:

- link your recognition records, feedback and game progress
- apply Deep Search allowances and daily limits
- protect the service from abuse

## Deep Search

- Deep Search runs only when you tap it. It sends OpenAI a couple of short excerpts from the transcript (about ten words each) so it can search the web. Emails, phone numbers and links are removed automatically first, but other spoken details like names may remain.
- Possible titles are looked up on TMDB. No transcript is sent to TMDB.
- We store the search and its result, including short quotes and the web sources it found.
- We keep a record of searches started on your device or account for 2 days, to apply the daily limit.

## Feedback and Requests

- When you tell us a match was right or wrong, report a miss, tap Correct, or tell us what a clip was, we store your answer with the recognition record.
- Title requests store the title you enter.
- Puzzle requests store what you enter, including the name and email address you provide, so we can follow up.

## Clypse Games

We store your puzzle attempts, guesses, scores, streaks and archive history so your progress and statistics work. They're linked to your device, or to your account while you're signed in.

## Clypse Account (Optional)

- You can use Clypse without an account.
- If you sign in with Apple, we store:
  - Apple's identifier for your account
  - the name you share
  - the email you choose to share (Apple can replace it with a private relay address)
  - which of your devices are signed in, recorded as one-way hashes of their identifiers
- While you're signed in, your saved clips (including the matched transcript) and saved quotes are stored on our servers so they sync to your other devices. Free accounts back up 50 clips and 50 quotes; Clypse+ backs up 2,000.
- Deleting a clip or quote removes its content from our servers on the next sync.
- Signing out stops syncing, and you choose whether to keep a copy on that phone.

## Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple. We never see your payment details. To give you Clypse+ or Deep Search credits, we store each purchase's App Store transaction ID, the product, its dates, and your credit balance, linked to your Clypse account.

## Sharing and Usage Events

- When you share a result, we create a link containing the title, artwork and matched quote. Anyone with the link can view it until it expires after 30 days.
- When you open a where-to-watch link, we record which service and title you chose, to understand which links are useful.

## Technical Information

- Requests include your app version and platform.
- Your IP address is used briefly to limit abuse and is not stored in our database. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs for a limited time.
- Notifications and Live Activities are created on your device; we don't collect push tokens.

## Who We Share Data With

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising or analytics tools. We share data only with providers that help us run Clypse:

- **OpenAI**: audio transcription, matching and Deep Search
- **TMDB**: title details, artwork and where-to-watch information (no personal data)
- **Apple**: Sign in with Apple and purchases
- Our cloud hosting and database providers

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

## How Long We Keep Data

- **Audio**: deleted right after it's transcribed.
- **Account details, synced library and purchases**: until you delete your account.
- **Recognition records, feedback, game progress and usage events**: kept to run and improve Clypse until you ask us to delete them.
- **Share links**: they stop working after 30 days.
- **Deep Search daily-limit records**: 2 days.

## Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time from the account screen. This removes:

- your account details and device links
- your synced library
- your Clypse+ record and unused Deep Search credits

It also revokes Clypse's access to your Apple ID. Two things stay:

- To stop free Deep Search allowances being reset by deleting and re-creating an account, we keep a one-way hash of your Apple sign-in identifier with your current allowance usage until that period resets.
- Records linked to your device identifier rather than your account (recognitions, feedback, games played while signed out) stay unless you ask us to delete them.

## Your Choices and Rights

- Use Clypse without an account, and turn microphone access off at any time in iPhone Settings.
- Delete saved clips and quotes in the app, and delete your account from the account screen.
- Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing [support@clypseai.com](mailto:support@clypseai.com). Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Session tokens are kept in your iPhone's Keychain, and identifiers are stored as one-way hashes where we don't need the original. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

## Children

Clypse isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

We'll update this policy when our practices change and show the new date above. We'll tell you about significant changes in the app.
