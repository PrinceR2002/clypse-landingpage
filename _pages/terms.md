## 2. The Service

Clypse helps you identify movies and TV shows from short audio clips, and offers daily puzzles (Clypse Games).

- Live Audio uses your microphone; Screen Capture records the audio of what's playing on your device. Clips are sent to our servers and our service providers to be transcribed and matched.
- Deep Search looks for titles beyond our database using web search and AI.
- You can save clips and quotes, share results with a link, and play Framed and Quotele.
